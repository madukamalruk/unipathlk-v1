# UniPath LK product requirements

Updated17 October2026. Primary audience: Sri Lankan post-A/L students across all fields. Collaboration is Sinhala; UI, code and product documents are English.

Help students compare education options against their actual interests, results, money, location, work and home responsibilities. Explain known constraints, missing facts and actionable institutional sources. A returned route is not an admission decision.

## Approved scope

1. Twelve domains and37 real programmes with independent identity, entry, fee, duration, delivery, recognition, progression and intake claims. Catalogue coverage remains limited.
2. Current A/L stream, subject grades/pass count, year, optional Z-score, O/L grades, prior qualifications, savings, monthly contribution, district, relocation and study preferences.
3. Graph-based direct/multi-stage discovery; prerequisites on both nodes and edges; branch isolation, cycles and search caps; deterministic eligibility/cash-flow authority.
4. At most three candidate pathways with a comparison table, conditions, evidence, unknowns, planning activity and read-only graph projection. Blocked options stay distinct.
5. Bounded Gemini planning and a separate optional contextual study guide. AI cannot create programme facts, numeric fees, accepted IDs or an admission outcome.
6. Another-A/L decision aid with separate scenario inputs: completed sittings, preparation months/costs, weekly study time, support, goal and income forgone. Compare present routes without changing actual grades or assuming future passes.
7. Compact responsive native Jac UI, Colombo clock, source disclosures, light/dark themes, keyboard support, explicit loading/error/fallback/stale states and a show/hide guide panel.
8. Local verification first, publication to madukamalruk/unipathlk-v1. JacHammer hosting remains deferred until owner review. Owner's final deadline:17:00 Asia/Colombo on17October2026.

## Exclusions

Accounts, saved profiles/plans, payments, loan approval, live scraping, a complete national database, foreign-scholarship matching, job guarantees, fabricated transfers, predicted grades/admission probabilities, invented income and resilience percentages. Do not add infrastructure solely because a generic security research document mentions it.

## Acceptance evidence

- Every domain has at least two sourced records; unknown complete costs stay null.
- No AI, edge or preference bypasses a failed prerequisite or cash constraint.
- Retake unknowns remain unknown; extra initial savings uses the whole preparation ledger. Foregone income stays separate and is never double-counted.
- Official retake/admission guidance is scoped by document/year. Historical candidate examples do not become current permission or an automatic attempt counter.
- Guide input/history and output IDs are bounded, provider calls opt-in, facts/links server-authored and text rendered plainly. No arbitrary tools or external URL fetching.
- Source checker, native/API tests, actual startup, exact-secret scan and scoped UI evidence recorded separately. Passing tests do not establish nationwide data correctness or full production readiness.
- Jac share reported with reproducible method; official counting interpretation remains to be confirmed.
